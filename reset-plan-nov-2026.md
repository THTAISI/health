## Why it broke

1. **Too much at once.** 5 sessions, up to 80 min each, 8 daily habits, 5 supplement slots, 4 fixed meals. Fine on a good week, collapses on a travel or launch week.
2. **No feedback loop.** Nothing logged = no proof it works = motivation dies around week 4–5. Classic.
3. **All-or-nothing.** One missed week turned into "off the plan" instead of "a 2-session week".

## The rule from now on

**A bad week is 2 sessions + the lunch bowl. Never zero.**
