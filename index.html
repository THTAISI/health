How weight goes up

  • Stop 2 reps short of failure on every set (RIR 2). Week 0 to 2: 3 reps short.
  • Hit the top of the rep range on all sets? Next time add 2.5 kg upper, 5 kg lower.
  • Pull-ups: negatives or assisted until 6 strict. Never to failure until the week 12 test.
  • ◆ marks width work (side delts, lats). That's what builds the model frame.
  • Rest: 90 to 120 s on big lifts, 60 s on raises and arms.

Running rules

  • Zone 2 = 125 to 140 bpm. Slow jog or run-walk in the heat is correct.
  • 2 easy runs + Barry's per week. Add max 10% time per week.
  • Before 08:00 or after 19:00. 500 ml + electrolytes before every run.
  • Barry's replaces the Saturday long run and is your interval session. Count ~500 kcal, don't eat it back.
  • No races, long hard runs, or running to burn off a dinner.
